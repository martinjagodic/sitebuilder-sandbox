## 4. Menu

### 4.1 What exists and where

* **The only complete menu with prices is on Wolt.** It has **8 categories and 66 items** (59 food items and 7 bottled drinks), plus option lists. It was taken from Wolt's public assortment API on **24 Sep 2026**. Wolt prices **include the 0,80 € packaging surcharge** on most items, so **dine-in prices are lower**. The dine-in menu is **not published online in full.**
* Wolt descriptions are copied verbatim. The repeated packaging sentence is removed from the description column and marked instead (✓ in "Opomba o embalaži"). The sentence reads: *"V ceno jedi je všteto doplačilo 0.80 eur za biorazgradljivo embalažo"*.
* Wolt shows descriptions only for some items. "—" means Wolt has no description.
* Wolt has no size variants. Every pizza has one price.
* **The dine-in menu has dishes that are not on Wolt.** The sloveniaholidays text and jedelbom's category list, from provider data updated 9 Aug 2026 ([jedelbom menu](https://jedelbom.com/menu-pizzerija-gorenc)), list these categories: *PIZZE IZ KRUŠNE PEČI, PIZZA KRUHKI, TESTENINE IN TORTELINI, LAZANJE (mesne, zelenjavne, mešane), ZREZKI, MESNE SESTAVLJENE SOLATE, LIGNJI Z ŽARA, DOMAČE SLADICE, OSTALE JEDI PO NAROČILU*. Wolt has no lasagne, tortellini or squid (lignji) dishes.
  * A Google reviewer (Hubert B.) recommends: "Ocvrti Lignji, Polnjeni Lignji Na Žaru, Dunajski Zrezek S Pomfrijem, Pizza Kebab, Palačinke Z Jagodnim Prelivom, Pizza Gorenc, Pica Z Articokami In Sparglji, Pohane Lignje, Špageti Bolognese, Domače Klobase V Testu 3 Kos, Kmečka Pica".
  * An Instagram reel of 8 Aug 2026 promotes kalamari ("Hrustljavi na zunaj. Mehki znotraj. Takšni, kot morajo biti pravi kalamari. 🦑").
  * An Instagram post of 9 Sep 2026 promotes ribs ("Rebra, pri katerih bonton hitro postane samo predlog. 😏🔥 Počasi pečena. Zapečena ravno prav."). They are not on Wolt.
* **A partial dine-in price glimpse** comes from an Instagram photo of the printed menu, 7 Jul 2025 ([post](https://www.instagram.com/p/DLzfwBNoztF/), `ig-menu-card-01.jpg`, 640 px, blurry). Legible fragments:
  * "OSTALE JEDI": "ZREZEK NA ŽARU s prilogo pommes ali zelenjava — svinjski / puranji", with prices 12,40 € / 12,90 € beside it.
  * Further lines for squid dishes ("…VRTI LIGNJI s tatarsko omako in s prilogo (pommes)", "…LIGNJI NA ŽARU", "…in sirom, blitva… tržaška omaka"), with prices between 11,90 € and 13,90 €, and 4,50 €. Which price belongs to which dish can't be read reliably.
  * "PIZZE": "KEBAB" with 12,00 € / 12,70 €; "PEKOČA" with 10,90 € / 11,50 €. Each pizza has **two price columns**, meaning unknown (sizes?).
  * The same pizzas cost 14,70 € and 14,10 € on Wolt in Sep 2026. **Don't use these fragments as prices.** Ask the owner for the current printed menu.
* The printed menu is designed in rust/terracotta on a parchment texture, with the logo. This is useful as a brand cue.
